# Binance USDⓈ-M Futures PAPER

This is a separate simulated account in `data/binance_futures_paper.sqlite3`. It does not read or alter the Spot, Adaptive, Matrix, Directional or earlier autonomous-agent ledgers. It never calls a signed/private Futures endpoint and contains no exchange order transport. `LIVE` is off.

The public collector calls only allowlisted GET endpoints on `https://fapi.binance.com`: `time`, `exchangeInfo`, `klines`, `ticker/bookTicker`, `ticker/price`, `depth`, `aggTrades`, `premiumIndex`, `openInterest` and, while positions exist, `fundingRate`. It starts with BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT and SOLUSDT USDⓈ-M perpetuals. A stale, missing or inconsistent snapshot pauses decisions. The [Binance USDⓈ-M market-data reference](https://developers.binance.com/en/docs/catalog/core-trading-derivatives-trading-usd-s-m-futures/api/rest-api/market-data) documents the public fields.

`positionSide` is LONG or SHORT; the synthetic MARKET execution side is BUY to open long/close short and SELL to open short/close long. OPEN consumes ASK or BID respectively, and CLOSE uses the opposite quote, plus configured adverse slippage. Maker orders are not simulated; a book touch alone cannot prove a maker fill. Fees and limits are explicit in `futures_paper_config.json`, not inferred from a user's Binance account. Starting capital is 100 USDT, gross exposure is capped at 75% of equity at entry, and effective leverage is reduced if market moves make it exceed 1x.

Contract price, mark price and index price are stored separately. Mark drives stop, unrealized PnL and approximate margin risk. Executable BID/ASK drives estimated liquidation and realized PnL. Settled funding is charged/credited exactly once from public `fundingRate` records using their associated mark price and `fundingTime`; the current indicative rate is *not* booked in advance. Closed-trade accounting reconciles `grossTradingPnL + fundingPnL − fees − spreadCost − slippageCost = netPnL`.

Initial/maintenance margin, margin ratio and estimated liquidation price are educational 1x PAPER approximations, **not Binance's exact tiered margin and liquidation algorithm**. No liquidation fee, insurance-fund execution, queue position or guaranteed fill is implied. The net-liquidation metric uses current bid/ask and configured fees/slippage; it is an estimate, not a realizable quote.

Six L1/L2/S1/S2/N1/N2 families generate research hypotheses. The central Futures agent can simulate long, short, keep, close, trailing protection and a partial cross-symbol BTC short hedge when positive BTC beta/correlation and estimated risk reduction exceed round-trip cost. It refuses same-symbol opposite positions. However, **no family currently has independently validated net-profit probability/OOS edge**, so production promotion is empty and new entries stay FLAT. Heuristic EV fields are flagged uncalibrated and must never be read as a forecast or a guaranteed return. No environment variable or HTTP endpoint can bypass promotion.

The new research engine stores closed candles, point-in-time proposals and matured 30-minute outcomes in the separate `data/edge_lab.sqlite3` database. Fixed chronological TRAIN (seven days), VALIDATION (three days) and subsequent OOS windows do not randomly reshuffle observations. The eight directional variants cover six L1/L2/S1/S2/N1/N2 families. A quote observed for both entry and exit can qualify for complete simulated costs only if no funding settlement falls inside the hold; missing quotes or a funding interval remain diagnostic and block promotion. At least 100 independent hourly OOS blocks, positive validation/OOS confidence bound, cost/fill stress, three symbols, three weekly periods and live shadow outcomes are required before automatic PAPER-only promotion. A negative recent 50-trade expectancy pauses a candidate. The engine cannot discover an edge instantly: initial state is `COLLECTING`, active candidates may legitimately remain zero, and model outcomes are not a profit guarantee.

The promoted model's exact directional signal is re-evaluated by the Futures PAPER operator; a different heuristic from the same family cannot inherit its evidence. Entry sizing is 10/20/30/40% tiers of current PAPER equity, further limited by stop risk, correlation, gross/net exposure and the hard risk veto. Existing accounts migrate only the former 25% per-symbol cap to 40%, with an audit event and without resetting wallet, positions or trade history. Stops remain immediate; take-profit and one 25% reduction wait at least five minutes to avoid early micro-profit exits.

KEEP/CLOSE/REDUCE decisions retain their reference mid-price. Later closed candles mature 5/15/30-minute counterfactual outcomes exactly once in the Futures PAPER ledger. The counts and fractional returns in `/api/autonomous-agent` are diagnostics of decisions, not realized USDT or proof that a hypothetical alternative could have filled.

`AGENT_PROVIDER=EXTERNAL` optionally enables an HTTPS JSON decision source with `AGENT_API_URL`, `AGENT_API_KEY` and `AGENT_TIMEOUT`; invalid JSON, timeout or failed risk checks fall back to the deterministic local operator. External output cannot add leverage, enable LIVE, invent a promoted model or bypass the bid/ask PAPER fill rules. The API key is never sent to Binance. Leave these variables unset for local-only operation.

Read-only APIs: `/api/futures-paper`, `/api/edge-lab/status`, `/api/edge-lab/candidates`, `/api/edge-lab/rankings`, `/api/autonomous-agent`, `/api/portfolio-risk`. `/api/edge-lab` remains the older legacy/Matrix diagnostic ranking. The watchdog supervises server workers with restart backoff. The app and research run only while the server runs; public Futures availability varies by network/region, and feed failure is reported rather than replaced with Spot data. Tests use synthetic Futures market data and do not require credentials.

Optional operator control: set a strong `PAPER_CONTROL_TOKEN` on the server, then POST `{"action":"PAUSE_ALL"}` or `{"action":"RESUME"}` to `/api/paper/control` with `Authorization: Bearer <token>`. Without the token, the endpoint is unavailable. PAUSE blocks new entries but keeps exit/risk management and research active; the pause state is persistent across restarts. This endpoint never enables real orders.
